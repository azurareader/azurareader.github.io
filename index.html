About AzuraReader

Welcome to AzuraReader, the ultimate smart browser designed exclusively for web manga, comics, and webtoon enthusiasts. Instead of displaying cluttered webpages, AzuraReader transforms them into a clean, immersive, and distraction-free reading experience.

Key Features

  • Smart Web Reader — Browse and load manga, comics and webtoon pages from your favorite websites with fast rendering.
  • Ad Blocker & Clean View — Automatically removes annoying advertisements and pop-ups while reading.
  • Advanced Reading Modes — Vertical Webtoon mode or horizontal page mode.
  • Bookmarks & Reading History — Continue reading exactly where you left off.
  • Dark Mode — Comfortable reading experience during day or night.
  • Fast & Lightweight — Optimized for smooth performance on iPhone and iPad.

Important Notice

AzuraReader is a specialized web browser and reading management tool. It does not own, host, upload, store or pre-load any manga, comic or webtoon content. All content is accessed directly from publicly available web pages provided by users.
