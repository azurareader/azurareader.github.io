Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Welcome to AzuraReader: Manga & Comics ("the Application"). We are fully committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our services.

1. Information Collection and Use

AzuraReader functions as a specialized web browser and reading progress management tool. We guarantee that:

  • No Personal Information Collected: The Application does not require any user account registration and does not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location).
  • Local Storage Only: All reading data, including your reading history, bookmarks, and interface configurations, is stored directly and exclusively on your local device. We do not upload or sync this data to any external servers.

2. Web Browsing Data and Cookies

Since the application loads content directly from public web URLs accessed by the user:

  • All cache data and cookies generated during browsing are handled locally by the native iOS system component (WebKit/WKWebView) to optimize loading speeds and maintain your session state on those websites.
  • We do not store, track, or monitor your web browsing history or online activities.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application does not integrate any third-party analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs. However, please note that when you load content from third-party websites, those sites may apply their own privacy policies and data collection practices. We highly recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the source websites you visit.

4. Device Permissions

The Application only requests standard iOS network permissions required to connect to the Internet. We do not request access to sensitive permissions such as Contacts, GPS Location, or Camera.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional improvements or legal updates. Any modifications will become effective immediately upon being posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via the developer support email provided on the App Store.
